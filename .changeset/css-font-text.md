Typed font and text values. `Font.parse` reads the `font` shorthand into its longhands (style, variant, weight, width, size, line height and family list), or a system font; `Font.parseFamily`, `parseWeight`, `parseStyle`, `parseSize` and `parseLineHeight` read the longhands. `Text.parseAlign`, `parseVerticalAlign` and `parseDecoration` (with `parseDecorationLine`, `parseDecorationStyle` and `parseDecorationThickness`) read `text-align`, `vertical-align` and `text-decoration`.
